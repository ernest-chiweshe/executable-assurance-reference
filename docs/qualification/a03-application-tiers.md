# A03 synthetic application tier composition

Candidate 038 integrates the local product_spec, Education and Health tier
contracts into one source tree. It remains a working candidate; the adopted
baseline is 035 and A03 remains OPEN.

product_spec preserves its default signed tier-three policy. Its explicit
synthetic tier-four fixture requires two distinct review principals. Education
uses a separate signed synthetic pack for tier-four release-feedback and keeps
the original confidence-driven additional-review condition. Health preserves
the historical workspace-membership profile and its requester-may-approve
rule: the owner can supply one grant, while synthetic tier four requires a
second distinct verified clinician and independent reporting lines.

The reviewed Health caller retains exact prepared inputs, original session
binding and artifact bytes, resolves stored review, then uses the existing
authorize/commit/settlement path. Incomplete quorum stays pending. Current
membership validation rechecks the original signed receipt before and after a
live lookup without reissuing it or extending its expiry. Membership receipts
are bound to review through the signed certificate's receipt digests.

All fixture identities, monitors, directories, documents and memberships are
synthetic. They do not establish professional licensing or deployment custody.
The ordinary post-grant deactivation contract changes private fixture state;
IdentityStore provides no public administration endpoint for that operation.
The check establishes aggregate refusal, not independent proof of each
redundant defensive branch. A peer's absent key-derived preparation lookup is
not SQL namespace/RLS isolation evidence. The preparation self-digest detects
corruption, not a hostile writer.

The local monitor_ingress login inserts signed clearance observations. It has
SELECT only on tenant and decision_record_id conflict-key columns, with no
clearance body/signature SELECT, UPDATE or DELETE. The receiver remains the
reader. Existing RLS and append guards remain in place. This division does not
by itself qualify monitor-process isolation.

Staged Windows evidence contains 34 product/Education/clearance contracts and
14 Health contracts, plus earlier preparation and offline checks. It is not a
substitute for integrated execution. Full audits require the complete retained
private candidate including owner records and publication drafts. Those files
remain outside distributable archives. No publication or deployment is authorized
by assembling this candidate.

Cumulative original-source provenance is pinned in
[adaptations](../provenance/a03-application-tiers/adaptations.json).
The clinical preparation extraction retains its original bytes and explicit
diff. The source trace verifies that relationship without claiming independent
acceptance. Frozen v12 authority and historical publication facts are unchanged.

Integrated Health contracts also include a synthetic host registry jurisdiction
change after signed review, while both subjects remain active and original
receipts remain current. This is a provider-configuration fixture transition,
not a public administration endpoint or professional-licence integration.
